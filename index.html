Assumptions & Method

Everything that underpins the numbers, and what still needs business confirmation.
Assumption to confirm #1 — the largest FMCG unit's target. Its FY27 revenue target (2,581 Cr) is far below its FY26 base yet is labelled a growth year. Confirm the FY27 scope (a line may be divested or excluded) before relying on this target; even on a declining trend the projection exceeds it.
Assumption to confirm #2 — portfolio total. The sum of the 16 SBU revenue targets is 11,845 Cr, but the summary deck quotes a lower headline total. Confirm which SBUs that headline covers before relying on the portfolio total.

Revenue basis

Revenue recognised on the general-ledger sales lines (local + foreign), credit entries. Freight and agency commission income are excluded — cost pass-through / commission, not marketing-driven sales. Same basis as the marketing-productivity model.

Marketing Revenue & Profit

Marketing Revenue = CY revenue − PY same-period revenue (like-for-like; Sep uses the same 9 days of Sep-25). Marketing Profit = Marketing Revenue × FY26 gross margin ((Revenue − COGS) / Revenue). No ROMI — this is a business-health view, not a media-efficiency view.

Forecast method (seasonal × trend)

  • Each remaining month = prior-year same-month × (1 + Q1-to-date YoY growth), preserving seasonality while carrying the current trend forward.
  • A YoY trend is only used when the prior-year Q1 is a genuine base (≥40% of current Q1); otherwise (new/ramp/pre-launch SBUs) the forecast is held flat at the recent Jul+Aug run-rate.
  • Trend is capped at ±100% to prevent explosive extrapolation.
  • Three scenarios: Repeat FY26 (0% growth) / Current trend / Plan (target).

Data caveats

  • Sep-26 is 9 days MTD; prior-year comparators are like-for-like.
  • The air-travel unit's revenue is volatile (ticket refunds), and its target conflicts with the marketing budget.
  • The commodities line has a negative FY26 gross margin and is winding down — its Marketing Profit is not meaningful.
  • Several units (healthcare devices, crop protection, real estate) are pre-launch (no material revenue yet).
  • GL actuals can exceed the budget-vs-actual "net sales" line because the GL is booked on gross billing.
